# Public prediction-market alpha: catalogue for the Susquehanna Predictions Cup (2026 midterms)

Compiled 2026-10-21. Format per edge: (a) mechanism, (b) evidence/numbers, (c) data needed, (d) transfer to a play-money SUSQies CLOB with a DMM ladder, (e) source. Tags: **[MEASURED-ON-SIG]** = measured on this very venue by a participant; **[MEASURED]** = measured on another venue; **[SPECULATIVE]** = argued, not measured.

Venue facts used throughout (from the OpenAPI spec and rules, all verifiable):
- 237 YES/NO exchanges across 117 races + 4 chamber-control markets; tick 0.005 on [0.005, 0.995]; no fees; peer-to-peer matching; sells of unbacked inventory are canonicalized to complement buys (`sell yes q@p` == `buy no q@(1-p)`); self-trade prevention cancels the crossing remainder; resting orders do NOT escrow cash; cash moves only on fills. https://sig.thesuper.market/api/v1/openapi.json
- Rate limits: 100 reads + 30 writes / min / account (all keys share one budget); DMM accounts get 600/200. Market-data REST is cached 1–2 s (orderbook 1 s). Realtime = Supabase private broadcast, 1-second batches carrying full versioned books for tournament markets; best-effort, no replay. https://sig.thesuper.market/api/v1/openapi.json
- Multi-leg: up to 10 legs atomic; `executionMode: "allOrNone"` runs every leg IOC and accepts only if all fill fully (the arbitrage primitive). Batch: up to 50 best-effort. `POST /orders/cancel-all` scoped by exchange for re-quoting. https://sig.thesuper.market/api/v1/openapi.json
- DMM endpoints exist (`/dmm/.../liquidity` append/replace rungs with retained queue priority; `DELETE` sweeps all `market_maker`-owned orders; `/dmm/.../inventory` reports the maker's synthetic inventory and worst-case loss). Rules: Sponsor "may act as a market maker in any or all Markets" with no obligation. https://predictionscup.com/rules/
- Leaderboard = cash + positions at a trade-based mark (last trade, not the book) **[MEASURED-ON-SIG]**; final ranking = settled balance only; prizes $30k/$5k/$2.5k; ties pool and split; bots allowed, one account per person; the Sponsor may alter outcomes/resolution, void trades; MNPI trading prohibited. https://predictionscup.com/rules/ , https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker
- Settlement: automatic from configured data sources every 4 hours; 4 chamber-control markets settled manually (closed to trading while awaiting); cancelled/N/A outcomes refund at cost. https://sig.thesuper.market/docs/settlement-and-payouts
- Leaderboard API: sort by pnl/roi/winRate/volume/trades, periods 1d/7d/30d/all (UTC calendar). Smart Score = time-decayed composite of PnL, ROI, win rate, Sharpe-scaled vol. https://sig.thesuper.market/api/v1/openapi.json

## 1. Susquehanna Predictions Cup: what participants have published

No Reddit/X/Discord strategy threads were found (searched "Predictions Cup", "SUSQies", "thesuper.market"). All public evidence is on GitHub (~20 repos). The single most informative source is a live-traded, instrumented bot with a written attribution analysis (grissou). Field size: 1,087 accounts on 4 Oct (<20% had traded; ~870 untouched at exactly 100k), 1,403 ranked on 12 Oct, 1,727 on 20 Oct.

### 1.1 The "tilt": a measured favourite–longshot bias on SIG vs Polymarket **[MEASURED-ON-SIG]**
- (a) Tournament mid m relates to Polymarket probability r by m ≈ c + (1−s)(r−c), c = 1/#candidates: prices pulled toward "all candidates equal" by factor s. Shorting longshots / holding favourites to settlement earns the tilt because every contract pays at the result. Mechanism offered: rank-chasing play-money participants buy lottery tickets; longshot sellers are capital-bound (a 10c short locks 90c of cash for 7c of edge).
- (b) s = 0.026 (1 Oct 12:00) → 0.052 → 0.083 (3 Oct) → 0.128 (4 Oct 12:00, peak) → ~0.10 (5 Oct) → 0.078 (6 Oct) → 0.092 (20 Oct). Sub-5c longshot best bid rose 3.1c → 10.1c; >95c favourite best ask fell 0.968 → 0.907 over 1–4 Oct. "A candidate with a 2% chance trades at 10–15c; one with a 97% chance trades at 85–88c." Weekends brought the strongest rises (+4.2 points 3 Oct noon → 4 Oct midnight), Monday mornings the falls; "weekly waves of 2 to 4 points on top of the trend." Edge per $ of collateral for a longshot short in a 2-leg race: s=0.05 → 2.4–2.5%; s=0.10 → 4.7–5.1%; s=0.15 → 7.1–7.9%; s=0.20 → 9.8–10.8%. Bid sums exceeded 1 in 82 of 117 races on 4 Oct (longshot bids deep, asks thin). Bot results at 20 Oct: 104,786 at marks (+4.8%), 114,487 expected value at settlement (+14.5%), rank 187/1,727; 12,981 fills 2–11 Oct averaging 5.3c/share edge (10.9% of cash tied up). Attribution 5–10 Oct: harvest ladder +2,885 on 19.4k sold (15% per unit cash); hourly reallocation +2,676 on 23.6k bought (11% gross, ~5% net of the sale legs); market making +786 realised on a 20k reserve; momentum sleeve −521 (sold 12.9k of value positions, bought nothing); refill selling −3,905.
- (c) SIG books (full depth), Polymarket mids per race (229 of 237 contracts match; use only where Polymarket spread ≤3c), cross-sectional regression every cycle (slope / WLS / median estimators; median is more robust to pinned tails). The tilt estimator must be re-estimated from "others-only" books because your own quotes sit at the top of tail books.
- (d) Directly transferable: it IS this venue. Caveats: a rising tilt marks tilt-selling positions DOWN on the leaderboard (a mark-driven stop rule gave away 3.7k of EV in 4 hours on 4 Oct); cash is the binding constraint (>90% deployed by day 3); correlated worst case must be capped (bot uses 40% of account; race-to-wave ρ 0.55, control markets 0.85).
- (e) https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker (README §3, §6, §7) ; tilt time series and path analysis: https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker/blob/main/analysis/reports/TILT_PATHS.md ; estimator: https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker/blob/main/analysis/reports/TILT_ESTIMATOR.md ; attribution: https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker/blob/main/analysis/reports/RETURNS_ATTRIBUTION.md

### 1.2 Complementary-party (NO+NO / "dutch book") arbitrage within a race **[MEASURED-ON-SIG]**
- (a) Each race lists separate D-win and R-win (sometimes I-win) binaries. At most one resolves YES, so NO on every leg pays ≥1 per set (2 if neither wins). Buy when Σ NO asks ≤ 1 − edge, i.e. Σ YES bids ≥ 1 + edge. YES+YES (Σ YES asks < 1) is only safe for exhaustive races (third-party risk; Idaho/South Dakota Senate flagged non-exhaustive by one repo). Unwind early when Σ NO bids ≥ 1 (sell the set back) to free capital.
- (b) Observed instances: Delaware Senate 30 Sep 2026, YES bids 0.95 + 0.08 = 1.03 (3% locked). nl2992 ticket example: Delaware SELL_ALL, 1,000 sets, 3.09% edge, breakeven last-leg NO ≤ 0.080. On 13 Oct, fresh paired books: Maine Senate and NY-17 House, 100 NO shares on each party cost 99.50 vs min payout 100 (0.5% edge); Senate-control pair cost 100.50 (no edge). grissou: "bid sums above 1 in 82 of 117 races" at the 4 Oct tilt peak. IamYanis audit 7 Oct: 117 title pairs found; a 0.0025/share cost per leg would erase the typical 0.005 pair edge. Scanner thresholds used by participants: 0.005 min edge (christiano), 0.5%/1%/2% tiers (IamYanis), ARB_EXIT_MIN_PROFIT 0.0025/set (benjamingao).
- (c) Only SIG books (`GET /exchanges/prices` bulk for 237 markets in 3 reads; full books for signalled races; book age ≤2 s at send), race grouping from titles / `GET /markets/{id}/nodes` (race ID, stage ID, party-winner resolution type). Execute with `/orders/multi-leg` + `allOrNone`.
- (d) Transferable and already crowded: several bots scan it every 5–15 s. Edges are small (0.5–3%) and capital locks to settlement unless unwound; ties up cash that the tilt trade uses better (grissou retired "arbitrage and pair unwinds" because reallocation at the touch did the same job). Still the only truly risk-free edge available and sizable at tilt peaks/weekends when longshot bids fatten.
- (e) https://github.com/nullif1ed/sigprediction ; https://github.com/nl2992/sig-arb ; https://github.com/eshan327/predictions-cup/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md ; https://github.com/christiano7777777/sig-arb-bot ; https://github.com/benjamingao93/SIG-prediction-bot ; https://github.com/IamYanis/Susquehanna-Prediction-Cup-Strategy

### 1.3 Cross-venue fair value: take SIG quotes that are far from Polymarket/Kalshi **[MEASURED-ON-SIG]**
- (a) Fair = Polymarket (or Kalshi/Polymarket average) probability, scaled within race to sum to 1; buy YES at ask a when (p−a)/a clears a hurdle; short via NO when (b−p)/(1−b) clears it; hold to settlement (no cross-platform cash transfer is possible, so this is value trading, not arbitrage).
- (b) grissou value book: edge per fill 5.3c/share over 12,981 fills; gaps at entry on tails of 5–7.5c (e.g. Dem Rhode Island Senate mid 0.917 vs Polymarket 0.992; Rep NH Governor 0.887 vs 0.96). Polymarket-vs-Kalshi agreement within ~1 point on typical races (988 matched races, samyakbaid). nullif1ed thresholds: 1.5pp net edge required (1pp "lost money out of sample"); sniping resting asks ≥3pp below FV (2pp "looked better in-sample but lost out of sample"); "directional trades and sniping lost money on the newest unseen data"; live mode defaulted to arbitrage only. Lnvqntx: min edge 15pp, 18 positions, +824 (+0.8%) by 12 Oct, rank 365/1,403. eshan327: RTWH Senate forecast 0.702 vs SIG Maine-D ask 0.580 (12.2pp raw gap; 0.072 after a 5pp haircut, −0.028 after 15pp).
- (c) Polymarket Gamma/CLOB public API and Kalshi public API (no account needed), a race-to-market crosswalk (one repo mapped 237 SIG exchanges to 693 Polymarket condition IDs: 140 EXACT, 87 DERIVED, 4 NEAR, 6 NO_TRADE), SIG executable depth (not displayed mid).
- (d) Transferable. Watch resolution-rule mismatches (Senate control includes caucusing independents, VP tiebreak, President-pro-tempore fallback; Alaska has candidate-only markets externally). Edge sizing by executable VWAP, and a hurdle that widens with |Kalshi − Polymarket| (3pp + 0.5×gap, +2pp if only one venue lists the race) was one participant's rule.
- (e) https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker/blob/main/analysis/reports/POLY_BIAS_RESULTS.md ; https://github.com/nullif1ed/sigprediction ; https://github.com/Lnvqntx/Susquehanna-Predictions-Cup ; https://github.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market (MATH.md §4–6) ; https://github.com/DanielCWeng/predictions-cup (crosswalk) ; https://github.com/eshan327/predictions-cup/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md

### 1.4 Hourly reallocation / capital rotation (sell least-edge holdings, buy most-edge levels) **[MEASURED-ON-SIG]**
- (a) With cash locked to settlement and >90% deployed, returns come from "buy the most edge per unit of cash, sell the least, never below value." Swap only if net edge improves ≥5 points; sale leg may go ≤3c below value; turnover cap 15k/hour; never sell the headline control markets; never flip long→short.
- (b) +2,676 EV on 23.6k bought (11% per unit cash gross; ~5% net of sale legs), almost all on one day with 20k of free cash. A rotation layer was independently identified as the bottleneck by Lnvqntx ("entry engine has deployed essentially the full bankroll").
- (c) Own positions + fair values + books. Pure venue data plus external fair.
- (d) Transferable; essential in a no-fee, settle-at-end game with a 100k cap.
- (e) https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker/blob/main/analysis/reports/RETURNS_ATTRIBUTION.md ; https://github.com/Lnvqntx/Susquehanna-Predictions-Cup

### 1.5 Market making on SIG: crowded, small **[MEASURED-ON-SIG]**
- (a) Quote both sides one tick inside the best other trader in the 15–85% band, never within 1c of fair, quarter-Kelly size, recycle inventory older than 6 h or >3k per market at a 1c concession.
- (b) "Within hours of the open several participants were quoting algorithmically: undercutting by one tick, re-quoting instantly, resting size at every level." Pure MM earned "tens of units a day on a 20k reserve"; +667 realised over 4,050 fills in 4 days but cost 1,627 of EV to fund; inventory held a median of 7 hours. By 13 Oct all 237 books were two-sided with median spread 0.005 and median best-ask quantity 2,905 shares. grissou: "the book became what was likely one of the most liquid in the whole of prediction markets: tighter than Polymarket's own." Pre-open the Sponsor's maker quoted ~8 points wide (e.g. Texas Senate D 54.5/62.5 vs external 58.5–60) [samyakbaid, pre-open observation].
- (c) Realtime books (note: the exchange stopped granting the tournament book channel on 12 Oct; only the user fills channel remained), REST reconciliation every 30 s, cancel-all re-quote, SIGUSR1 handover restart to keep queue position.
- (d) Transferable but low-yield now; the DMM ladder is no longer the marginal quote in most markets. Keep as a small sleeve or only in thin tails where the tilt provides the edge (the "harvest ladder": asks at +0/+2/+4/+6c above the touch on ≤10% longshots, bids below on ≥90% favourites, 3k per level, ≥8% edge per $ — the best earner once funded).
- (e) https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker ; https://github.com/eshan327/predictions-cup/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md ; https://github.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market

### 1.7 Momentum in the tilt / copying leaders: lost money **[MEASURED-ON-SIG]**
- (b) A "momentum sleeve" that bought longshots while the tilt was rising went live 7 Oct 16:00, sold 12.9k of value positions to fund itself, bought nothing before the tilt turned, −521 EV. Author: "in a market it is usually a bad position to be responding to someone else making more and trying to copy them." Path analysis: a 5% longshot bought at 10c and sold later returns −41% if s→0.03, −10% at 0.10, +35% at 0.20; path-weighted ≈ +7% with 35% chance of losing a third or more.

### 1.8 Leaderboard marks are trade-based: a measurement hazard (and a rules risk) **[MEASURED-ON-SIG]**
- (a) The exchange values positions at the last trade, not the book. "A single trade in a thin longshot book moves the mark by cents and the account by hundreds, with no effect on settlement." Contracts marked at 15% traded at 5% on Polymarket. On 1 Oct market 387's bids swung 0.96 → 0.16 → back as bots pulled a whole side of the book; a naive stop sold into it.
- (d) Implication 1: manage to EV-at-settlement, not to marks; stops on marks are harmful. Implication 2: during the first 37 hours trade-based marks put one account anywhere between 100k and 150k. Deliberate mark manipulation would fall under "unsportsmanlike or disruptive conduct" / void-trade powers in the rules; not an edge to pursue.
- (e) https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker/blob/main/docs/ENGINEERING_NOTES.md ; https://github.com/nullif1ed/sigprediction ; https://predictionscup.com/rules/

### 1.9 Tournament-structure sizing (convex payoff) **[SPECULATIVE, simulation]**
- (b) samyakbaid tournament sim (1,500 rivals, 116 real races, shared national wave, ρ assumed 0.5): 3rd place needs ≈4.5× start (~450k); random P(top 3) = 0.2%; bot-only small-edge strategy 0.0% top-3 (avg 105k); 4 cheap same-direction longshots (8–25%) bought 3 pts below fair → 6.0% top-3, 63% chance of finishing <100k; without the 3-point edge 3.3%. Market-implied correlation from Kalshi's Senate seat ladder (P(≥16)=76%, P(≥18)=50%, P(≥20)=18.5%) is ρ ≈ 0.15, so treat the 4.5× as illustrative. Wave betas from 6,168 races 1998–2024: Senate 0.87, House 0.74, Governor 0.70 per point of national swing; historical fundamentals-only national surprise SD ≈ 6 points. tanaymihani pre-registered sim (1,000 players, 40 markets, visible leaderboard): winner median 92× start (10–90th pct 19×–691×), third place median 35×; 1× Kelly reached top 3 in 0 of 10,000 contests; "0.5× Kelly, all-in whenever outside top 3" P(top 3) 2.15%, expected prize $271 vs $37.50 even split, median bankroll 0.
- (c) Own ranking vs leaderboard (`GET /tournaments/{slug}/leaderboard`), race correlation model, fair values.
- (d) Transferable in shape but not in level: actual leaderboard on 20 Oct had a +4.8% account at rank 187/1,727 and a +0.8% account at 365/1,403 on 12 Oct, so the real top-3 threshold is unknown (query `/leaderboard?sort=pnl&limit=3`). Both sims agree that top-3 requires concentrated correlated longshot exposure late, not Kelly growth. Keep 15–20k for the final 72 hours/election night (grissou).

### 1.10 Operational facts that are edges in themselves **[MEASURED-ON-SIG]**
- Reads ≈0.4 s from the exchange's region (San Francisco), 1–1.5 s from the UK. Day-one order writes took 14–30 s; `409 REQUEST_IN_FLIGHT` on same-key retries. 429 carries `Retry-After: 60` (one minute blackout). Writes at 45/min caused penalties; 28/min was safe. The realtime feed drops with code 1006 a few times a day and does not auto-reconnect. 20-minute outage on 7 Oct. Full-universe book sweep took ~201 s at 100 reads/min, so a sweep is not a simultaneous arbitrage snapshot — use `/exchanges/prices` (bulk, ≤100 IDs per call) to screen and fetch full books only for signalled races. Reducing a NO holding as a covered "sell NO" needs no cash; sending it as "buy YES" was refused hundreds of times a day at 100% capital.

## 2. Polymarket / Kalshi published arbitrage and market-making strategies

### 2.1 Within-market rebalancing and NegRisk/combinatorial arbitrage on Polymarket **[MEASURED]**
- (a) YES and NO (and the N outcomes of a NegRisk event) are priced separately; buy all YES when Σ < 1 (long arb) or all NO / split-and-sell when Σ > 1 (short arb). Combinatorial: logically dependent markets (winner vs margin buckets) priced inconsistently.
- (b) Saguillo et al. (AFT 2025): 8,659 single-condition + 1,578 NegRisk markets (17,218 conditions) resolving Apr 2024–Apr 2025, ~86M bids. 7,051 of 17.2K conditions had ≥1 opportunity (all long, Σ<1, when no token >$0.95); 662 of 1,578 NegRisk markets had opportunities averaging ~100 per market with average max profit ≈40c per $. Realized ≈$39.6M total: single-condition $5.90M (buy <1) + $4.68M (sell >1); multi-condition NO-long $17.31M, YES-long $11.09M, YES-short $0.61M. Top account $2.01M over 4,049 transactions; top-10 accounts $384K–$2.01M each; one trade earned ~$58,983 buying YES+NO each <$0.02. Only ~1% of estimated US-election single-condition opportunities were exploited. Cross-market dependent pairs: 13 confirmed US-election pairs, 0–6,630 opportunities each, avg max profit ≈$100 (low liquidity), realized ≈$95K across 4 pairs. Opportunities cluster in volatile, uncertain, still-liquid periods (Biden withdrawal in August, November election).
- Tsang & Yang (2026): YES+NO deviation half-life (AR(1) on 5-min bars) in the Trump market fell to 0.74 min (Oct 2024) and 0.67 min (Nov); Harris >30 h in Mar–Apr while thin; Kyle's λ for Trump YES 0.53 (late July) → 0.04 (Sept) → 0.01 (Oct): a $1M net buy moved p by ~13 points at λ=0.53 vs ~0.25 points at 0.01; once ADV > ~$1M, λ fell an order of magnitude.
- NegRisk executable vs payoff parity (Tiger/Polymarket Substack summarising a paper): the adapter only converts NO→YES, so NO-side violations close fast (36 episodes, median 7.99 s) while YES-side violations are frequent and persistent (2,098 episodes, median 16.15 s); $1.12M reconstructed arb profit, 97% via converter strategies, top-10 addresses ≈75%.
- (c) Order books + trade tape per outcome; on SIG the analogue is the race-level complement (1.2) since YES/NO of one exchange are mechanically complementary.
- (d) Partially: SIG's within-exchange YES/NO cannot diverge; race-level NO+NO can (1.2). Half-life results tell you these gaps close in under a minute when bots compete — on SIG multiple bots already scan every 5–15 s.
- (e) https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03474 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2508.03474 ; https://collective.flashbots.net/t/arbitrage-in-prediction-markets-strategies-impact-and-open-questions/5198 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03136v2 ; https://tigerpolymarketprediction.substack.com/p/weekly-algorithm-executable-vs-payoff ; https://docs.polymarket.com/advanced/neg-risk.md

### 2.2 Cross-venue (Polymarket vs Kalshi vs Betfair) **[MEASURED]**
- (b) 2024 presidential: execution-adjusted Polymarket–Kalshi spreads averaged ~$0.03 pre-election-night, sustained intervals ~$0.07; a chunk is semantic (Polymarket resolved on network calls, Kalshi on inauguration) plus regulatory segmentation; a naive "take the highest-yield equivalent-market arb" strategy across venues returned 1,218.66% cumulative over 800 days in 15 trades (2022 onward). ### 2.3 Open-source market-making bots **[design patterns, no P&L published]**
- poly-maker (~1.5k stars): fair value = depth-weighted microprice adjusted by an EWMA of signed trade flow; reservation price r = FV − inventory skew; half-spread = base + volatility term + toxicity (per-fill markout EWMA) term; quotes BUY-YES at r−h and BUY-NO at (1−r)−h so a filled pair merges to collateral at a locked edge; regime machine QUIET (farm rewards band) / TRENDING (lean, widen, half size) / EVENT (pull quotes after a sweep or jump, cool off) / REDUCE_ONLY (inventory cap or near end date) / HALTED (stale data, resolution, kill switch); reconciler uses churn tolerances; dead-man heartbeat; per-market, neg-risk-group worst-case, total exposure and daily-loss caps.
- alexmann3k: fixed 5-share quotes, no Avellaneda–Stoikov, preserves existing orders to retain queue position.
- Polymarket CLOB is price-time priority (best price, then earlier order); GTC/GTD/FOK/FAK order types.
- (d) Transferable mechanics: microprice + flow EWMA, toxicity-widening, event-pull, queue-preserving requotes (SIG's DMM `replace` endpoint explicitly retains unchanged rungs' queue priority; a participant bot keeps queue position across deploys via handover restart). SIG has no post-only or FOK for single orders, but `allOrNone` multi-leg is IOC.
- (e) https://github.com/warproxxx/poly-maker ; https://github.com/alexmann3k/polymarket_mm_bot ; https://www.mintlify.com/Polymarket/clob-client/concepts/trading ; https://docs.polymarket.com/concepts/order-lifecycle

### 2.4 Kalshi liquidity-incentive program: how a venue shapes the ladder **[MEASURED (program terms)]**
- (b) Snapshots once per second at a random moment; per period: Target Size (100–20,000 contracts per side), Discount Factor (≤1.00), Reference Price = first level walking down from best bid where cumulative resting size reaches 1/5 of Target Size; order score = size × DF^(ticks away from reference); normalized per side per snapshot (max 2.0 per snapshot across all participants); snapshot excluded unless BOTH sides meet Target Size; rewards capped at $10,000/business day on the most volatile markets. Kalshi's 2025 contract terms require a minimum order size of 5,000 contracts for some participants (IEM–Kalshi paper). Fees were $0.07·P(1−P) per contract to takers only until April 2025.
- (d) Explains why real-money DMM ladders are deep near the touch with geometric decay; SIG's sponsor ladder pre-open was ~8 points wide and has since been out-competed by student bots to a 0.005 median spread, so DMM-ladder sniping is not the main game on SIG (no rewards program exists on SIG).
- (e) https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823851-liquidity-incentive-program ; https://www.karlwhelan.com/Papers/Kalshi.pdf ; https://www.biz.uiowa.edu/faculty/trietz/papers/IEM2026Forecasts.pdf

### 2.5 Copy-trading top wallets **[MEASURED, mostly vendor data]**

### 2.6 News/feed latency ("sniping stale quotes") **[MEASURED anecdotally]**
- (b) Reported sports bot made ~$8M in two months on Polymarket by trading the gap between live data feeds (~+3 s) and the public livestream (~+45 s), attributed to server proximity (unverified, secondary). "The Sniper's Tax": a stale YES offer at $0.22 vs fair $0.99 loses ~77c; sniping losses "can be 80 cents on the dollar" vs a few ticks in traditional markets; MM expected profit E[π] = (s/2)·V(s) − P_news·L_snipe; MMs respond by widening or pulling ("liquidity mirage"). Betfair enforces in-play delays (football 5–9 s, tennis 1–3 s, racing 1 s) so resting orders get hit on goals before cancellation; Betfair cross-matching can jump FIFO queue priority.
- (d) Transferable in two directions on SIG: (i) election night: AP calls/county counts move before most students react, and the 1–2 s REST cache + ~0.4 s read latency bound how fast anyone can act; (ii) defensively: pull/widen quotes around scheduled news (debates, major polls) since "fills keep losing money right after news" was a participant's documented failure mode (raise MAKE_EDGE to 0.03). Campaign-period news on 237 down-ballot races is slow, so the sniping edge is concentrated in the last 72 hours.

## 3. Statistical anomalies in prediction markets

### 3.1 Favorite–longshot bias: magnitudes by venue **[MEASURED]**
- Kalshi (Bürgi, Deng & Whelan 2025/26; >300,000 contracts): contracts <10c lose >60% of money; 5c contracts win ~2%; 95c contracts win ~98% with small positive return; average pre-fee return per contract ≈ −20%; takers lose ≈32%, makers ≈10%; a 20% true probability maps to a ~23% average belief; a 0.2-priced contract pays with probability ≈0.18; accuracy improves toward close; bias shrinking over time. https://cepr.org/voxeu/columns/economics-kalshi-prediction-market ; https://www.karlwhelan.com/Papers/Kalshi.pdf
- Kalshi calibration (independent, 12,970 prices, 1,979 events): slope β = 1.29 [1.21,1.39] at 1 h before close, 1.07 [0.98,1.18] at 1 day, 1.08 at 7 d, 1.07 at 30 d; restricting to 0.10–0.90 at 1 h gives β 1.05 [0.91,1.21]; high-volume (>500k contracts) markets β 1.54 one day out (exploratory); backing sports favorites loses 0.42c mid, 2.38c after spread+fees; "nothing is exploitable by a trader who takes liquidity"; the gain at the 1-cent floor goes to makers. https://github.com/nalimmm/kalshi-calibration
- Polymarket (Cardozo & Rivero-Wildemauwe, 588M trades, 2.48M accounts): purchases <10c lose 19.3c per $; ≥90c earn 0.83c per $; sign of longshot result flips with grouping (equal-weight contracts −6.3c, parent-event grouping +4.1c); present in Crypto and Politics, absent in Sports; heavy favorite buyers earn less than others. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12878
- Polymarket weather: longshot overpricing mainly at distant horizons; favorite overpricing strengthens in the final 1–12 h; simple recalibration no significant gain. https://arxiv.org/html/2610.13593
- Intrade (Page & Clemen 2013, >500k transactions, 1,787 markets): slope 1.25; bias of 4.7–10.9 pp; well calibrated near expiry, significantly biased far out (>100 days for elections); near-efficient in sports. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1468-0297.2012.02561.x
- PredictIt (Zitzewitz 2026, NBER w35845): longshots priced 0.20 paid 0.13 (yes/no) and 0.11 (two-outcome); election markets slope 1.84 vs 1.31 non-election; bias did NOT decline from 16 weeks to 1 day before elections (unlike Intrade), attributed to the $850 position cap binding arbitrageurs; bid sums persistently >110 (up to 125) in multi-outcome markets; arbitrage available in 1.9% of markets at any moment 2019–22 and persistent; underpricing (asks <100 in exhaustive markets) <0.1% of the time. Implication for SIG: a 100k cap with 237 markets is a position-limit regime; expect PredictIt-like persistence of the bias. https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w35845/w35845.pdf
- Horse racing / theory: Snowberg & Wolfers (2010) attribute FLB to probability misperception (prospect-theory weighting), not risk-love. https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w15923/w15923.pdf
- IEM: no FLB in 44 political WTA markets in the last 100 days (Gruca & Rietz 2026a), consistent with bundle arbitrage anchoring. https://www.biz.uiowa.edu/faculty/trietz/papers/IEM2026Forecasts.pdf
- (d) On SIG the bias is measured directly (1.1) and is larger than any real-money venue (2% events at 10–15c vs Kalshi's 5c→2%). The literature says: it persists under position limits, is largest in politics, and fades near resolution on venues without limits — so expect it to persist until the last days on SIG, with a possible final-72-hour spike (grissou path A: s 0.15–0.20) or collapse (path C/D).

### 3.2 Election markets underprice favourites (prices compressed toward 50) **[MEASURED]**
- Erikson & Wlezien (POQ 2008): 1992–2004 Intrade/IEM winner-take-all prices vs poll-based projections — polls' win projections were more accurate 87% of days (>90% with weekly averages); "the market greatly overvalues longshots"; WTA price slope on vote-share expectation flatter than the poll-based S-curve, i.e. the market overestimates campaign-shock variance. https://statmodeling.stat.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Erikson-and-Wlezien-POQ-2008.pdf
- Rothschild (POQ 2009): debias with Pr = Φ(1.64·Φ⁻¹(price)) (Leigh/Wolfers/Zitzewitz transformation estimated on 1880–2004 presidential markets); debiased Intrade beat debiased 538 polls in 2008, especially early and in not-certain races (Presidential + Senate). https://academic.oup.com/poq/article/73/5/895/1868587
- Le (2026), 353M trades / 429k contracts on Kalshi + Polymarket: "persistent underconfidence in political markets, where prices compress toward 50%"; replicates on Polymarket; large political trades on Kalshi associated with further compression (calibration-slope gap ≈ one-half). https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.19520
- Zitzewitz (NBER w35846, 1880–2025): no partisan overpricing (rules out left bias >4.5pp or right bias >3pp one day out); FLB present in most political markets; polling-average markets show ~1.4-point left overpricing. https://www.nber.org/papers/w35846
- 2022 midterms: PredictIt leaned Republican vs 538 (Oz 63% favorite); 538 had lower error at all horizons; lean helped in 2018/2020, hurt in 2022. https://arpitrage.substack.com/p/loss-for-prediction-markets-in-the ; https://statmodeling.stat.columbia.edu/2022/11/16/how-good-are-election-prediction-markets/
- (d) Supports a structural "long favourites / short longshots, hold to settlement" book on SIG and a debiasing transform for any SIG-price-derived fair value; the 1.64 factor is for real-money markets — SIG's own s-estimate is the venue-specific version.

### 3.3 Momentum vs mean reversion **[MEASURED, mixed]**
- Kalshi 2026 congressional-control contracts show significant day-to-day momentum on own lag (coefficients 0.129 p=0.013, 0.138 p=0.007, 0.261 p<0.001) while IEM shows none; neither venue Granger-leads the other; day-to-day changes across venues largely uncorrelated. https://www.biz.uiowa.edu/faculty/trietz/papers/IEM2026Forecasts.pdf
- Clinton & Huang (2024 cycle): daily changes "weakly correlated or negatively autocorrelated." https://ideas.repec.org/p/osf/socarx/d5yx2_v1.html
- Sports: Moskowitz finds open-to-close momentum and value in >100k contracts, reversed by the outcome (delayed overreaction), too small to beat costs; soccer pre-game odds show time-series momentum (underreaction). https://w4.stern.nyu.edu/finance/docs/pdfs/Seminars/moskowitz_seminar_paper.pdf ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S221480432300040X
- On SIG: trade-level lag-1 autocorrelation −0.19 to −0.29 = bid-ask bounce, not alpha (1.6); the tilt trends over days with weekend waves (1.1) but trading that trend lost money (1.7).
- (d) Weak. The one exploitable "momentum" is the slow multi-day drift of the tilt, best monetised by staged selling (harvest ladder) rather than by buying longshots.

### 3.4 Time-of-day / weekend effects **[MEASURED weakly]**
- Polymarket 2024: participation concentrated 09:00–20:00 UTC; top-10% traders peak 14:00–17:00 UTC. https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03136v2
- SIG: tilt rose most on weekends, fell Monday mornings; new accounts 2.7/h on Sunday vs 6/h on Friday; longshot bids fattest when retail is active (1.1). https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker/blob/main/analysis/reports/TILT_PATHS.md
- Fed paper on Kalshi macro markets documents "rich intraday dynamics" and sharp news reactions but no calendar effects usable here. https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/files/2026010pap.pdf
- (d) Schedule tilt-selling (resting asks on longshots) into weekend sessions; schedule capital raising on Monday mornings.

## 4. Election markets in the final 2–3 weeks

- Arbitrage/cross-venue gaps peak in the last two weeks (Clinton & Huang). https://ideas.repec.org/p/osf/socarx/d5yx2_v1.html
- Late 2024 Polymarket swings: Trump 67% → 53% → 62% within days (Axios). https://www.axios.com/2024/11/05/prediction-markets-2024-presidential-election-trump-harris
- Final-day flows: Harris YES net inflow turned negative on the last day; October 2024 Trump net inflow $176M, Harris $106M; rolling correlation of Trump and Democratic-side net inflows rose from 0.17 to 0.76 in early October (coordinated hedging/arbing). https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03136v2
- Election night 2022 example: Polymarket PA Senate fell from 61% to 0% six hours after the race was called (stale pricing on the losing side). https://www.playusa.com/news/2022-election-prediction-gambling-market/
- Favourites priced 85–95%: Kalshi 95c wins ~98%; Polymarket ≥90c earns +0.83c/$; PredictIt bias did not shrink into election day; IEM shows no bias in the last 100 days. Expect SIG favourites at 85–88c with true ≈97% (1.1) — the largest measured gap of any venue.
- Correlated races: no published quantification of same-state Senate/Governor mispricing; practitioner notes only (Georgia Senate/Governor linkage; Nevada/NH). Market-implied ρ ≈ 0.15 from Kalshi's seat-count ladder; historical wave betas Senate 0.87 / House 0.74 / Governor 0.70. SIG lists separate party markets per race AND chamber-control markets, so a chamber-control vs sum-of-races consistency check is possible but unpublished (eshan327 found the Senate-control NO pair cost 100.50 — no edge on 13 Oct). https://polycopy.app/polymarket-midterms ; https://github.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market ; https://github.com/eshan327/predictions-cup/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md
- Election-night model used by a participant: P(leader wins) ≈ Φ(m/σ) with m = current margin + expected shift from uncounted votes, σ shrinking with count; "if SIG still shows D at 60% when counts imply 75%, that's a 15-point edge." https://github.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market

## 5. Bot-level CLOB edges

- Queue position: Polymarket and IEM are price-time priority; Betfair cross-matching can jump FIFO; Kalshi LIP scores resting size by distance from reference; on SIG the DMM `replace` endpoint keeps unchanged rungs' order IDs and queue priority, and a participant bot keeps queue via SIGUSR1 handover (plain restart cancels everything). Queue-aware fill simulation turned a +0.33 bps "touch fill" signal into −1.64 bps in equities (tanaymihani). https://github.com/tanaymihani/markout
- Spread capture on SIG: median spread 0.005 (one tick) with ~2,905 shares at best ask (13 Oct); earlier (pre-open) DMM quoted ~8 points wide; MM realised +667 per 4 days on 20k. The spread is gone; capture only in tails where the tilt provides edge.
- Stale DMM/other quotes after news: the only documented SIG events are liquidity pulls (market 387 bids 0.96→0.16→back on 1 Oct) and the Rhode Island night where one persistent longshot buyer absorbed 27k of shorts. Protect with "pause a market after a sudden move," never risk ~1 to earn ~1c at the extremes, one-sided quoting where Polymarket and the book disagree by >5c.
- Rate budget is the scarce resource: 100 reads/30 writes per minute; bulk price endpoint covers all 237 markets in 3 reads; one bot budgeted 80 reads/28 writes; writes include cancel-all and batch (one each), so batch 50 orders per write.
- Cash semantics: resting buys do not escrow cash, so quotes across exchanges can sum to more than cash (engine shrinks/cancels on fills); covered "sell NO" to reduce a NO position needs no cash.

## 6. Ranked edges for this competition (plausibility × size, given the evidence)

1. **Sell the tilt and hold to settlement** (short longshots ≤10%, long favourites ≥90% vs Polymarket-scaled fair; harvest ladder of resting orders +0/+2/+4/+6c beyond the touch; ≥8% edge per $ collateral). Measured on SIG: s ≈ 0.09–0.13 → 5–8% per $ of collateral; 15% per unit cash on ladder fills; +14.5% EV at day 20. Risks: leaderboard marks move against you while held; cash lock; correlated wave (cap worst case ≈40%).
2. **Race-level NO+NO (and exhaustive YES+YES) arbitrage** via `allOrNone` multi-leg. Measured: 0.5–3% edges, 82/117 races with bid sums >1 at tilt peaks; crowded by scanners; unwind when Σ NO bids ≥ 1 to recycle cash.
3. **Cross-venue value taking** (SIG executable vs Polymarket/Kalshi) with ≥1.5–3pp hurdles widened by venue disagreement, sized by executable depth. Measured 5.3c/share average edge per fill; 1pp thresholds lost out of sample.
4. **Hourly capital reallocation** (sell least edge, buy most edge, ≥5-point net improvement, never below value floor). Measured 11% gross / ~5% net per $ rotated; the binding constraint in a 100k, no-fee, settle-at-end game.
5. **Final-72-hours / election-night speed** (AP calls, county counts; Kalshi re-pricing) with 15–20k held back. Supported by cross-venue arbitrage peaking in the final two weeks (2024) and stale losing-side pricing (PA 2022); SIG latency ≈0.4 s + 1–2 s cache; speculative but structurally sound.
6. **Tournament-convexity sizing**: concentrate late into a few correlated same-direction cheap contracts bought below fair; simulations give 2–6% top-3 vs 0.2% random, at a 63–97% chance of finishing below start. Check the real top-3 threshold on the leaderboard API before choosing the aggression level.
7. **Tail-only market making with queue preservation and event pulls** (band 15–85 is crowded at a 0.005 tick; tails carry the tilt edge). Measured small: tens of units/day on 20k in the middle band.
8. **Defensive microstructure knowledge** (treat marks as noise; no stops on marks; no imbalance/mean-reversion takers; pull quotes on sudden moves and around news). Negative results measured on SIG save money rather than make it.

Not transferable or disproven: copy-trading leaders (lost money on SIG), tilt momentum (lost money), bid-ask-bounce mean reversion (−0.2 to −0.3 lag-1 is not alpha), Kalshi/Polymarket rewards farming (no program on SIG), NegRisk adapter tricks (no equivalent), cross-venue cash arbitrage (no bridge).
