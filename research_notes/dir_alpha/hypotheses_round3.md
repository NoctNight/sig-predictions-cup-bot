# Round 3 ideas (31 Oct 04:45 UTC). All run on the existing panel, frozen rules unchanged.
Aim: ways to use the lag signal without new capital or the partisan cap, ways to make it a bigger trade, and sharper filters.
1. Timed offload: everything is sold by 18:00 ET 3 Nov anyway. Sell a leg when the signal says it will fall, hold it when the signal says it will rise. No new capital, no cap.
2. Rich-first offload: sell legs where SIG is above outside first.
3. Basket: when many legs show a 1c+ gap the same way, buy them all, small each. One big trade spread over many books.
4. Cluster: does a signal in a same-way cluster do better or worse than an isolated one?
5. Mirror check: the other leg of the race shows the same gap.
6. Big moves: outside moved 5c+ in 24 h (MT-01 +14c, FL-07 -10c). Is the leftover bigger, or does the outside revert?
7. Outside quality: outside spread and depth at trigger. Tight and deep means a real move.
8. Which venue led: Polymarket first or Kalshi first.
9. SIG-only moves: SIG moved, outside flat. Fade them.
10. Trigger hour: signals overnight against US hours, does the wait pay?
11. Long-short: pair a D-side signal with an R-side one so net D stays flat. Cap-free, drift-free.
12. Exit when the gap closes, instead of on a clock.
13. Mid band and SIG spread at trigger.
14. No tape for hours (stale prints, not stale quotes).
15. Final days in 2022 and 2024 outside prices: does the last 4-day move carry into election day?
16. Seat holder: D-held against R-held seats, open against incumbent, within the House.

## Results (31 Oct 05:15 UTC)
1. Timed offload: dead. About $100 to 3 Nov, our holdings sit in legs that rarely signal.
2. Rich-first: dead. No pattern in what our legs do by how rich they are.
3. Basket: first look +2.1c above the rest (3 baskets). Strict mechanics: +1.4c, +0.8c above the rest, placebo +0.5c, D-way only. Dead as a separate trade.
4. Cluster: same-way cluster adds nothing. Against a wave of 5+ loses: -0.6c to -1.4c (train and test negative in the 1.5c cut). Keep as a filter.
5. Mirror check: nothing.
6. Big moves: bigger is a little better. Already in the conviction count.
7. Outside quality: wide outside spread 2c+ gives +0.3c on the slow rule (9 trades). Weak. Depth: nothing.
8. Which venue led: nothing.
9. SIG-only moves: fade loses at every horizon to 24 h, and so does following them.
10. Trigger hour: slow 12-17 UTC +0.1c, fast 12-17 UTC +2.4c. Opposite signs, treat as noise.
11. Long-short: 18 pairs of 86 trades, +1.37c a trade, little less risk. Fits the cap, too few pairs to matter.
12. Exit when the gap closes: clearly worse than the clock (0.5-1.0c against 1.9c, fast 1.2-1.9c against 2.1c).
13. Own-side price 70c+: -0.1c on 13 slow trades, train and test both negative, rest +2.0c. Also true on loose events it was not found on (+0.1c, -0.1c). Keep as a filter. SIG spread: nothing.
14. Tape age: nothing.
15. Final days in past elections: not testable, one national path a year.
16. Open seat, partisan lean, ratings disagreement, previous signal in the race, leg's past slowness, gap widening or narrowing, our own MM activity: nothing.
