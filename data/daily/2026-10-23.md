## 4. What went live

**One change: recycle sizing fix (commit 359cc8c), deployed 11:36-11:39 BST.** Trading was paused for about 3 minutes while
the bot restarted (its resting orders are cancelled on stop and re-posted on start).
- What it does: before unwinding a held arb set to fund a better one, the bot now dry-runs the purchase it would make with
  the freed cash (same budget rules: per-race cap, capital cap, cash reserve, book depth) and shrinks the unwind until at
  least 95% of the freed cash would actually be spent. It also judges the swap on locked profit gained minus locked profit
  given up on the capital really moved, not on the two return rates alone.
- How it was tested: 7 behaviour tests replaying this morning's episode (tests/test_guards.py), a new fake-venue scenario
  `recycle_oversize` that fails on the old code and passes on the new (tests/sim_pipeline.py), the full 15-scenario
  simulation including the 24-hour replay (0 violations), an independent Fable review (SAFE TO DEPLOY), the deploy
  precheck, and a 10-minute post-deploy watch (result at the end of this section).
- Side effect to expect: fewer recycles. The reviewer noted that if recycles stop entirely, the first thing to check is
  whether committed arb capital exceeds the capital cap (then every follow-through is short and the fit refuses).
- Roll back: `git revert 359cc8c` then `deploy/mac/botctl.sh deploy`.
- Post-deploy watch 11:40-11:50 BST: passed. Settlement-basis value 102,445 -> 102,479, no unwinds, no kill switch, no tracebacks. Recycles stopped because cash is now at the 20k reserve and the fit refuses swaps the follow-through cannot spend; this is expected (see backlog item 1 for what to watch).
